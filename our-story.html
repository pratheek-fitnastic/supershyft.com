Meet RISHI!

The founder of FITNASTIC

And this is his inspiring story...

2008

A Shaken Childhood

Rishi was born in Jaipur on 6th September, 1995. He lost his father to oral cancer in 8th standard. The pain was deep. It taught him young - HEALTH is everything.

Diagnosed, Not Defeated

In 10th standard, he was diagnosed with Non Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease While others chased marks, he chased healing. He chose discipline over despair - HEALTH became his goal

2010
2011

Loss All Around

He saw more family members fade - an uncle, an aunt. Each loss felt personal, like a warning. He began to wonder - Why WAIT till it’s too late?

Another Setback

In 2013, an accident fractured his left hand. A rod was inserted. He couldn’t work out freely - His STRENGTH was tested again.

2013
2018

The Comeback Begins

He rose again, quietly and steadily. Graduated with B.Pharma + MBA from NMIMS. A silent promise burned inside - To create IMPACT.

The Corporate Life

He joined Cipla - handling cancer marketing and strategy. Life had come full circle - back to a world of pain and loss. Rich or poor, cancer didn’t spare anyone. Everyone fought disease, But few worked to PREVENT it.

2018
2019

A Hard Truth

People spent lakhs on cure, but ignored prevention. He realized fitness wasn’t enough - it had to start with health. That sparked the idea of FITNASTIC.

Health Over Comfort

He quit his job. No safety net. But he chose purpose over paycheck HEALTH over Fitness became his mission

2019
2019-
2021

Starting From Zero

With no family business, he taught piano to kids. Each class funded his dream. The VISION never left his heart.

Fitnastic Is Born

In 2022, he launched Fitnastic. Not just a fitness app - a preventive health movement. Backed by BELIEF. Backed by IIT BOMBAY RESEARCH.

2022
Since
2022

Just The Beginning

What started with one story became a community Fitnastic now inspires thousands. Because REAL WEALTH begins with GOOD HEALTH.
