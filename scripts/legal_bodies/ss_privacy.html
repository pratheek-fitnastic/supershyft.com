This Privacy Policy explains how SuperShyft collects and protects personal information. Related: Terms & Conditions.

This Privacy Policy explains how Fitnastic Health Pvt. This Privacy Policy applies to all individuals accessing or using the App, including individuals participating in corporate wellness programmes.

By accessing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

Depending on the services used, SuperShyft may collect the following categories of information:

2.1. Personal and Identification Information

  • Name;
  • Age or date of birth;
  • Gender, where voluntarily provided or required for a service;
  • Mobile number;
  • Email address; and
  • Other information necessary for registration and account management.

2.2. Health and Diagnostic Information

  • Health assessment responses;
  • Medical history and health-related information voluntarily provided;
  • Blood test and diagnostic reports;
  • Laboratory results and investigation records;
  • Relevant health measurements and wellness information; and
  • Other information necessary to facilitate diagnostic and wellness services.

  • Diagnostic test bookings;
  • Appointment dates and times;
  • Sample collection addresses;
  • Service history;
  • Payment status and transaction references; and
  • Cancellation and refund records.

  • Device type and operating system;
  • IP address;
  • App usage information;
  • Log data;
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information; and
  • Other technical information generated through the use of the App.

2.5. Communication Information

Information shared through customer support requests, emails, complaints, feedback and other communications with SuperShyft.

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect personal information:

  • Directly from Users during registration and use of the App;
  • Upon Users booking diagnostic tests, appointments or other services;
  • Upon Users uploading or submitting health information and diagnostic reports;
  • From laboratories, diagnostic centres and service providers involved in fulfilling a User's booking, where lawfully permitted;
  • Automatically through device and application technologies; and
  • Through communications with our customer support team.

Where information is obtained from a third-party service provider, SuperShyft shall process such information in accordance with applicable law and the purposes for which the information is made available.

4. Purpose of Collection and Processing

SuperShyft may collect and process personal information for the following purposes:

  • Creating, maintaining and managing User accounts;
  • Providing access to diagnostic, health assessment and wellness services;
  • Scheduling appointments and facilitating sample collection;
  • Processing, storing and displaying diagnostic reports;
  • Maintaining service and transaction records;
  • Providing health-related information, wellness insights and lifestyle recommendations available through the App;
  • Communicating booking confirmations, updates, reminders and service notifications;
  • Processing payments, refunds and billing;
  • Responding to complaints, queries and support requests;
  • Improving the functionality, performance and security of the App;
  • Preventing fraud, misuse and unauthorised access;
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements; and
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Personal information shall not be processed for purposes unrelated to those communicated to Users unless such processing is permitted under applicable law.

5. Health and Diagnostic Information

5.1. Supershyft recognises that health and diagnostic information is particularly confidential and requires appropriate safeguards.

5.2. Such information may be processed to facilitate diagnostic services, display reports, maintain health records and provide the health and wellness features available through the App.

5.3. Diagnostic information may be received from laboratories and other diagnostic service providers involved in fulfilling a User's request.

5.4. Access to health-related information shall be restricted to authorised personnel and service providers who require such access for legitimate operational purposes.

5.5. Supershyft shall not disclose identifiable health information to unrelated third parties for their independent commercial use without an appropriate legal basis and, where required, the User's consent.

5.6. Users must ensure that they upload or provide only information that they are authorised to share.

6. Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Information

Supershyft may share personal information, where necessary and legally permitted, with the following categories of recipients:

6.1. Diagnostic Laboratories and Service Providers

Relevant information may be shared with laboratories, diagnostic centres and sample collection partners to facilitate bookings, sample collection, testing and report delivery.

6.2. Technology and Infrastructure Providers

Information may be processed by hosting providers, cloud infrastructure providers, software vendors, analytics providers and other technology partners engaged to operate, maintain and secure the App. Such providers shall be required to handle information in accordance with applicable contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations.

6.3. Payment Service Providers

Limited transaction and payment-related information may be shared with payment gateways and financial service providers to process transactions, refunds and payment verification.

6.4. Corporate Clients and Employers

Where Supershyft provides services through an employer or corporate wellness programme:

  • Employers shall receive only aggregated and anonymised information, including overall participation statistics, consolidated health trends and general wellness insights.
  • Employers shall not receive individual diagnostic reports, personal medical histories, individual test results, names linked to health information or other personally identifiable health information.
  • Individual employees' personal and health-related information shall remain confidential and shall be processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.
  • Supershyft shall take reasonable measures to ensure that aggregated information does not enable the identification of individual employees, including through small-group reporting or the combination of datasets.
  • An employer's sponsorship, payment for or facilitation of a corporate wellness programme shall not, by itself, entitle the employer to access an employee's individual health information.
  • Aggregated reports shall be prepared in a manner that reasonably prevents the identification of individual employees.

6.5. Legal and Regulatory Authorities

Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, court orders, regulatory directions or lawful requests from competent authorities.

6.6. Professional Advisers

Information may be shared with legal, audit, accounting and other professional advisers where necessary and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

7. Data Storage, Third-Party Service Providers and Security

7.1. Supershyft may engage third-party service providers, technology partners and infrastructure providers to facilitate the operation, maintenance and delivery of services through the App.

7.2. Such third-party service providers may include:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers responsible for storing and maintaining personal information;
  • Diagnostic laboratories and sample collection partners involved in processing diagnostic requests and reports;
  • Technology and software providers responsible for application development, maintenance and technical support;
  • Payment gateways and financial service providers involved in processing transactions;
  • Analytics and application monitoring providers engaged to assess and improve App performance;
  • Doctors, registered medical practitioners, qualified healthcare professionals and nutritionists empanelled with SuperShyft to provide consultation, health guidance, nutritional assessments or dietary recommendations through the App; and
  • Other service providers engaged for legitimate operational and service delivery purposes.

7.3. Third-party service providers may process personal information on behalf of Supershyft to the extent necessary for providing their respective services. Empanelled doctors, registered medical practitioners, healthcare professionals and nutritionists may access relevant personal, health-related and diagnostic information, where necessary, to provide consultations or services requested by the User and subject to applicable consent requirements and confidentiality obligations.

7.4. Doctors, healthcare professionals and nutritionists empanelled on the App shall provide consultations in their individual professional capacity and shall be responsible for ensuring that their advice, recommendations and services comply with applicable professional standards, qualifications and regulatory requirements. Their empanelment shall not, by itself, create an employer-employee relationship with Supershyft. Supershyft shall take reasonable steps to verify the relevant qualifications and credentials of such professionals.

7.5. Supershyft shall ensure that appropriate contractual confidentiality, security and data protection obligations are imposed on third-party service providers handling personal information on its behalf, as required under applicable law.

7.6. Supershyft shall implement reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Such measures may include access controls, authentication mechanisms, encryption where appropriate, secure infrastructure, monitoring and restricted employee access.

7.7. Access to personal and health-related information shall be limited to authorised personnel and service providers, including empanelled professionals, who require such access for legitimate operational purposes or to provide services requested by the User.

7.8. Supershyft shall take reasonable steps to ensure that third-party service providers handling personal information on its behalf are subject to appropriate contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

7.9. In the event of a personal data breach, Supershyft shall take appropriate remedial measures and make notifications to affected individuals and authorities as required under applicable law.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

8.1. Personal information shall be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and contractual retention requirements.

8.2. Diagnostic reports and associated health information may be retained for the period necessary to provide the relevant services and comply with applicable record-keeping obligations.

8.3. Upon account deletion or withdrawal of consent, SuperShyft shall cease the relevant processing and erase personal information where required by applicable law, subject to lawful retention requirements.

8.4. Information retained for legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention or other lawful purposes shall be appropriately restricted and protected.

8.5. Where personal information is no longer required for its intended purpose and no legal or other lawful retention obligation applies, it shall be securely deleted or anonymised, as appropriate.
