CURA Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 12-11-2026Last Updated: 12-11-2026

CURA Terms & Conditions SuperShyft Terms SuperShyft Privacy

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to information processed through CURA and supplements the general SuperShyft Privacy Policy. Related: CURA Terms & Conditions.

1. Introduction

1.1. This Privacy Policy explains how Fitnastic Health Pvt. Ltd. (“SuperShyft”, “Company”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, accesses, processes, uses, stores, discloses and deletes personal information through CURA, its AI-powered health information and report interpretation chatbot (“CURA” or “Chatbot”).

1.2. This Privacy Policy applies specifically to information processed through CURA and supplements the general Privacy Policy applicable to the SuperShyft application (“Platform”), wherever relevant.

1.3. This Privacy Policy should be read together with the CURA Terms & Conditions.

1.4. Where consent is required, SuperShyft shall obtain such consent through an appropriate affirmative action. Use of CURA shall not be treated as a substitute for any separate consent required under applicable law.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Depending on the manner in which you interact with CURA, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

2.1. Personal Identification and Account Information

  • Name, age and date of birth;
  • Email address and mobile number;
  • Account identifiers and relevant profile information; and
  • Other information associated with your SuperShyft account.

2.2. Health and Medical Information

CURA may process health-related information voluntarily provided by you or made available through your SuperShyft account, including:

  • Blood test reports and diagnostic laboratory reports;
  • Test results, biomarkers and other laboratory findings;
  • Medical history and health-related information;
  • Symptoms, health concerns and conditions disclosed during conversations;
  • Dietary habits and lifestyle information; and
  • Other health-related information relevant to your interactions with CURA.

2.3. Uploaded Documents and Reports

Users may upload diagnostic reports and other health-related documents, including reports generated by laboratories or diagnostic service providers other than Supershyft. Such documents may contain personal identifiers, medical information and other information relating to the User or another individual.

2.4. Chat History and Interaction Data

  • Questions and prompts submitted to CURA;
  • Responses generated by CURA;
  • Previous conversation history, where required to provide contextual responses;
  • Information shared during follow-up conversations; and
  • Technical interaction data relating to the use of CURA.

2.5. Technical and Device Information

We may collect relevant technical information, including device identifiers, IP addresses, application usage information, timestamps, diagnostic logs and technical error reports, to the extent required for operating, maintaining and securing CURA.

3. Access to Information Available on SuperShyft

3.1. CURA may access relevant health information already available within your Supershyft account to provide contextual and personalised responses.

3.2. Such access shall be subject to the permissions and consent provided by you through the Supershyft Platform.

3.3. The information accessed may include diagnostic reports, test results, health records and other information made available within your account.

3.4. CURA shall access and process such information only to the extent necessary for providing its intended functionalities and in accordance with applicable permissions and legal requirements.

4. Purposes of Data Processing

We may process your information for the following purposes:

  • To understand, interpret and respond to queries submitted to CURA;
  • To analyse diagnostic reports and provide AI-generated explanations of test results;
  • To generate dietary, lifestyle and general health-related information;
  • To retrieve relevant health information from your SuperShyft account;
  • To maintain conversation continuity and contextual relevance;
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot and improve CURA's functionality and performance;
  • To detect misuse, security incidents, fraudulent activity and unauthorised access;
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations; and
  • To address User requests, complaints and grievances.

Personal information shall not be used for purposes unrelated to the above without an appropriate legal basis and, where required, your consent.

5. AI Processing and Third-Party Service Providers

5.1. CURA uses artificial intelligence infrastructure and models to process information and generate responses. Supershyft owns and manages CURA's architecture and may engage external AI service providers and technology infrastructure providers to facilitate its functioning.

5.2. Depending on the AI models and integrations deployed by Supershyft, such third-party providers may include OpenAI, Anthropic or other technology providers.

5.3. Where an external AI service provider is engaged, information transmitted for processing may include:

  • User queries and prompts;
  • Relevant portions of conversation history;
  • Extracted text or information from uploaded reports;
  • Contextual health information required to generate a response; and
  • Other information strictly necessary for the relevant AI functionality.

5.4. The information transmitted to an AI service provider shall depend on the functionality being used and the technical configuration of the relevant integration.

5.5. Supershyft does not use User conversations, uploaded reports or documents submitted through CURA to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence models.

5.6. Supershyft shall maintain appropriate contractual and technical arrangements with its engaged AI service providers to reflect the restriction on using such User information for model training or fine-tuning, subject to the applicable service agreements and configurations.

5.7. We may also engage:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers responsible for hosting and maintaining CURA;
  • Technology and software providers responsible for application development, maintenance and technical support;
  • Security and diagnostic service providers responsible for system monitoring and troubleshooting; and
  • Other service providers reasonably necessary for the operation of CURA.

5.8. Such providers may process information on our behalf or in accordance with their applicable contractual arrangements and privacy commitments.

5.9. The use of external AI infrastructure may involve processing by service providers operating in jurisdictions outside India. Any such processing or transfer shall be undertaken in accordance with applicable Indian laws and relevant contractual and technical safeguards.

6. Disclosure and Sharing of Information

6.1. Supershyft does not sell Users' personal information as a standalone commercial product.

6.2. We may disclose or share information with:

  • AI service providers for processing User queries and generating responses;
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers;
  • Technology and software partners engaged in maintaining CURA;
  • Professional advisers, auditors and legal consultants, where necessary; and
  • Government authorities, courts, regulatory bodies or law enforcement agencies where disclosure is required or authorised by law.

6.3. Supershyft does not share User conversations, uploaded diagnostic reports or health-related information submitted through CURA with diagnostic laboratories for independent processing or commercial purposes.

6.4. CURA may interpret reports generated by independent diagnostic laboratories. However, such interpretation does not involve transmitting the User's information back to those laboratories for verification or processing by them.

6.5. We shall endeavour to ensure that third-party service providers receiving information are subject to appropriate contractual confidentiality, security and data protection obligations.

6.6. We shall not disclose personal information to unrelated third parties for their independent commercial purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

7. Storage, Retention and Deletion

7.1. Supershyft follows defined retention periods for information processed through CURA.

7.2. Uploaded diagnostic reports, medical documents and other files submitted through CURA shall be retained for a period of sixty (60) days from the date of upload.

7.3. CURA conversation histories, including associated interaction records, shall be retained for a period of sixty (60) days from the date of the last update to the respective conversation.

7.4. Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, an automated deletion process configured by Supershyft shall delete the respective records.

7.5. Users may independently delete individual conversations, uploaded reports and associated records through the available deletion functionality. Deletion of individual records does not require the User to delete or deactivate their Supershyft account.

7.6. Where a User independently deletes a record before expiry of the applicable retention period, Supershyft shall initiate deletion of the corresponding record in accordance with its deletion procedures and applicable legal requirements.

7.7. Certain information may nevertheless be retained where required under applicable law or where necessary for legitimate legal, security or dispute-resolution purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7.8. Information that remains in technical backups, disaster-recovery systems or third-party infrastructure following deletion may be subject to separate deletion cycles and applicable contractual retention arrangements. Such information shall not be retained or used beyond what is permitted by applicable law.

8. Data Security

8.1. Supershyft implements reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information processed through CURA against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.

8.2. Such measures may include access controls, authentication mechanisms, encryption, secure transmission protocols, system monitoring and restricted access to personal information, as applicable.

8.3. Access to User information shall be limited to authorised personnel and service providers who require such access for legitimate operational purposes.

8.4. In the event of a personal data breach, SuperShyft shall take appropriate remedial measures and comply with applicable notification and reporting obligations.

9. User Rights and Control Over Information

Subject to applicable law, Users may exercise the following rights in relation to their personal information:

  • Request information concerning personal data processed by Supershyft and the relevant processing activities.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent previously provided for processing personal information. Withdrawal shall not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken before such withdrawal.
  • Raise complaints concerning the processing or handling of personal information.
  • Exercise applicable nomination rights in the event of death or incapacity, where provided under applicable law.

Users may also delete individual conversations and uploaded reports through the available functionality without deleting their SuperShyft account.

Requests relating to personal information may be submitted using the contact details provided in this Privacy Policy. Withdrawal of consent may affect CURA's ability to access certain health records or provide specific functionalities.

10. Information Relating to Children

10.1. Access to the Supershyft Platform and CURA is restricted to individuals aged eighteen (18) years and above. Individuals below eighteen (18) years are not authorised to independently register for or use the Platform.

10.2. Notwithstanding the above, a parent or lawful guardian may, through their own authorised account, upload diagnostic reports or health-related documents concerning their minor child for informational assistance through CURA.

10.3. Where information relating to a minor is submitted by a parent or lawful guardian, CURA may identify such information as involving a higher-risk medical context and recommend consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

10.4. Such AI-generated responses shall not constitute medical review, diagnosis or validation by a doctor or healthcare professional.

10.5. The parent or lawful guardian submitting such information is responsible for ensuring that they are legally authorised to provide the relevant information and consent to its processing.

10.6. Supershyft shall process information relating to minors in accordance with applicable data protection requirements, including any additional parental consent, verification or safeguards required by law.

11. Cookies and Technical Tracking

CURA may use cookies, software development kits, analytics tools or similar technologies, where applicable, to maintain functionality, improve performance, monitor usage and enhance security. Users may manage applicable device or browser permissions through their respective settings, subject to the effect such restrictions may have on certain functionalities.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

12.1. Supershyft may modify or update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in CURA's functionality, technology, data processing practices or applicable law.

12.2. Material changes shall be communicated through the Platform or other appropriate channels.

12.3. Where required by applicable law, SuperShyft shall obtain fresh consent or provide an appropriate opportunity to Users before implementing material changes.
